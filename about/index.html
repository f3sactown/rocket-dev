About Rocket

Q Planner

Rocket helps a Q plan a workout, run it, and then write and share the backblast. It's made for F3 and FiA PAX, and it's free.

What you can do

  • Plan a workout section by section, with saved templates and exercises from the F3, FiA and region Exicons, all in Rocket's Exicon.
  • Run it with a timer on each thang: countdown, stopwatch, AMRAP, Tabata or EMOM.
  • Write the backblast from the workout, with your region's AOs and roster, and copy or share it ready for Band and Slack.
  • Discover other Qs' public workouts, like them and reuse them.
  • Look up PAX: their posts and Qs in each region, and their public workouts and backblasts.

Your data

Finished workouts and backblasts are public to signed-in PAX unless you make them private; drafts and private ones only you can see. Your region's sheet supplies the AOs, roster and post counts. You can export everything you have, import it back, or reset your account, in Settings.

v0.1.0-alpha.4Nov 6, 2026›
Features
  • Regions: "All F3 regions" or "All FiA regions" for PAX who post across several: every such region's AOs and PAX in the form, totals added up, and an "At … AOs" group per region on Discover. Each region has its own Library color, the same for everyone.
  • Organization: Settings chooses F3 or FiA (and with it the app's words), locked to your region's.
  • Home: Today's workouts (tap to run), Future workouts, and Trending as a sideways row.
  • Discover: sideways rows of tiles with each author's region, a My workouts section, and a one-time choice to include your own workouts.
  • Workouts: the original author is credited on reuses; a Needs backblast badge on your past workouts without one; links on Run workout open; no Announcements section.
  • Backblasts: only for your own workouts; Announcements is the backblast's own; Third F is gone and Second F is Coffeeteria (CoFiA for FiA); copying or sharing adds a link to the workout.
  • Library: its own page, region Exicons, the new FiA Exicon sheet, alphabetical tags.
  • Your data: export, import and reset in Settings.
  • Account: forgot password; the Rocket Q Planner logo.

v0.1.0-alpha.2Nov 4, 2026›
Features
  • Home: a new first page with your posts and Qs, the sheet's last update date, your drafts, and the most recent workouts and backblasts.
  • Run workout: the whole workout in three text sizes, with timers (Countdown, Stopwatch, AMRAP, Intervals, EMOM) that beep through the last 10 seconds and pin to the top when scrolled away, music links, and the screen kept awake.
  • Thangs: several per workout, each with an optional timer and music, separated by blank lines in the output with songs shown as 🎵 Title 🎵.
  • Workouts: private Notes for the Q, Publish workout, Reuse to start a new workout from an existing one, and defaults for every section's templates.
  • Backblasts: made from a workout with an editable copy of its sections, and can't be finished before the workout's date.
  • Settings: turn the launch animation off; menu icons in the accent color.
v0.1.0-alpha.1Nov 3, 2026›
Features
  • Workouts: plan a workout first (Basics, Introduction, Warm-o-rama, The Thang, Notes, Announcements, Words of Wisdom), then create its backblast from it with one button. The backblast copies the workout's sections onto its Workout page; each keeps its own Notes.
  • Backblasts: step-by-step editor with autosave, drafts, standard sections, saved and default templates, typed attendance (downrange, FNG, 2.0), several Qs, a Notes section, and Event Data / Workout Details / COT layout; Copy and Share add a plain PAX list at the end.
  • Regions: Google Sheets regions synced nightly into Supabase, with AOs by weekday (filling location, time and next date), roster suggestions, regulars and post / Q totals.
  • Lists: swipe to archive or delete with Undo, an Archive page, and Search across workouts and backblasts with highlighted matches.
  • App: F3 / FiA wording, light / dark / system themes and accent colours, rocket artwork and launch animation, installable app, menu navigation, settings, and a build number in the footer.
